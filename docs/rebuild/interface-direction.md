# Finance workspace direction

Mode: Operate. Audience: owner of two truck–trailer earning pairs, reviewing weekly settlements and business cash. Retain the existing React application, typography and navigation while the new accounting workspace runs alongside legacy reports.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One as-of cash answer followed by its period earnings explanation. Reserves and financing must be visibly distinct from incurred expenses.

OWN-WORLD: Established light operational interface; system sans, dark blue-grey text, restrained green for reconciled actions, amber for incomplete evidence. Dense tables belong in workflows, not repeated across Home.

STORY: Check available cash, understand where freight went, compare pairs, resolve exceptions, inspect one trend. Every amount has evidence and a reporting basis.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Company navigation and dates precede a two-column available-cash reconciliation. Revenue distribution follows. Mobile stacks the reconciliation and keeps tables within horizontal scroll containers.

FORM: Owner-approved five-section structure; code-led implementation within the existing visual system. No concept seed or image comp was requested; the approved plan fixes the composition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

No raster assets ship. Browser captures use an explicitly labeled synthetic business, not live cash data. Cash availability is a stock; accrual earnings are a period measure. Missing evidence must remain visible. Accountant package availability depends on readiness checks.
